Privacy Policy for Branch Fall

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Branch Fall is an iOS app made by Frances Dalton. It calculates the slope of a fixture branch on the phone. It does not track you.

Branch names, lengths, and falls stay on this iPhone. The app does not create an account, does not sell anything, and does not send the branch list to Frances Dalton.
