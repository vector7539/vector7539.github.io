Cribbage Peg Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Cribbage Peg is a free iOS app made by Walter Harmon. It keeps a two-player cribbage play on the phone. Game names, player names, the street, and the pegs stay on that iPhone.

The app does not require an account. It does not sell anything. It does not collect data for the developer. It does not use advertising or analytics.
