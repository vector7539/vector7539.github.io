Privacy Policy for Bolt Pitch

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Bolt Pitch is an iOS app made by Louis Lawson. It marks bolt centers on a plate on the phone. It does not track you.

Plate names, lengths, diameters, and centers stay on this iPhone. The app does not create an account, does not sell anything, and does not send the plate list to Louis Lawson.
