Rafter Layout Support

Rafter Layout is a free iOS app. Enter the span, pitch, lumber, seat, and overhang, then read the common-rafter cut sheet. A birdsmouth deeper than one quarter of the depth, or a seat shorter than 1.5 inches, is not written. An overhang over 24 inches, or less than 3.5 inches remaining, is not written. Roofs stay on this iPhone.
