Privacy Policy for Flight Rise

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Flight Rise is an iOS app made by Arthur Prescott. It divides a stair rise into equal risers on the phone. It does not track you.

Flight names, rises, and goings stay on this iPhone. The app does not create an account, does not sell anything, and does not send the flight list to Arthur Prescott.
